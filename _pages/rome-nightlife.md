Rome after dark is sociable, outdoors and late. A Roman night usually starts with dinner around 9, then moves to a square or a bar, and clubs fill up after 11. We organise the night so you meet people, see the best areas and get home safely.

## Our night tours

- **Rooftop sunset and aperitivo**: a terrace over the historic centre at golden hour, then a short walk through lit-up Rome.
- **Bar crawl with a local guide**: three or four bars, local drinks, games and stories, in Campo de&rsquo; Fiori and Trastevere or in the Monti district. Non-alcoholic options on request.
- **Club night in Testaccio and Ostiense**: the area with the most clubs and live music, with entry arranged by the guide.
- **Rome by night on foot**: Trevi, Pantheon and Piazza Navona without the daytime crowds, ideal for photos.
- **Summer by the sea and the Tiber**: in summer many clubs move to the coast and the river banks.

Tours are for adults only (18+). Prices and dates on request.

## Where young people go out in Rome

- **Campo de&rsquo; Fiori and Piazza Navona**: the classic place to start, bars spilling into the square.
- **Trastevere**: pubs, live music and street life until late.
- **Monti**: trendy bars and a relaxed crowd.
- **Testaccio**: the main clubbing area, on Via di Monte Testaccio. Many clubs open around 11 pm and some close on Sundays.
- **Ostiense**: former factories turned into clubs, bars and cultural spaces, with an alternative feel.
- **San Lorenzo**: the student district, cheap and lively.
