Rome has more to see than anyone can fit in one trip. Below is the full list of places travellers visit, grouped by area so you can plan a day on foot. Want it organised for you? [Ask for a tour]({{ '/contatti/' | relative_url }}) and we build the route.

## Ancient Rome

- **Colosseum**: the amphitheatre of the gladiators, the symbol of the city.
- **Roman Forum**: the political and religious centre of the Roman Empire.
- **Palatine Hill**: the imperial palaces, with views over the Forum.
- **Circus Maximus**: the ancient chariot-racing stadium, today a large park.
- **Baths of Caracalla**: the ruins of one of the biggest public baths in Rome.
- **Appian Way and the Catacombs**: the ancient road, tombs and underground burial galleries.
- **Domus Aurea**: Nero&rsquo;s Golden House, visited with a guide.
- **Trajan&rsquo;s Market and the Imperial Forums**: the oldest shopping complex in the world.
- **Capitoline Hill and the Capitoline Museums**: Michelangelo&rsquo;s square and ancient sculptures.
- **Mouth of Truth**: the famous stone face in Santa Maria in Cosmedin.
- **Theatre of Marcellus**: an ancient theatre next to the Jewish Ghetto.
- **Largo di Torre Argentina**: the ruins where Caesar was killed, today a cat sanctuary.

## Vatican and Prati

- **St Peter&rsquo;s Basilica and the dome**: climb up for a view over the whole city.
- **Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel**: Michelangelo&rsquo;s ceiling and the Raphael Rooms.
- **St Peter&rsquo;s Square**: Bernini&rsquo;s colonnade.
- **Castel Sant&rsquo;Angelo**: Hadrian&rsquo;s mausoleum, later a fortress and a museum.
- **Ponte Sant&rsquo;Angelo**: the bridge lined with Bernini&rsquo;s angels.

## Historic centre

- **Pantheon**: the best-preserved ancient building in Rome.
- **Piazza Navona**: Bernini&rsquo;s Fountain of the Four Rivers.
- **Trevi Fountain**: toss a coin to return to Rome.
- **Spanish Steps** and Piazza di Spagna.
- **Campo de&rsquo; Fiori**: market by day, lively square at night.
- **Piazza Venezia and the Vittoriano**: the monument to Victor Emmanuel II, with a panoramic terrace.
- **Jewish Ghetto**: history and Roman-Jewish food.
- **Sant&rsquo;Ignazio** with its painted ceiling, **Santa Maria sopra Minerva** and **Palazzo Altemps**.
- **Via Giulia** and **Via Margutta**: quiet, elegant streets.

## Tridente, Trevi and the Quirinale

- **Piazza del Popolo** and its twin churches.
- **Ara Pacis** and the **Mausoleum of Augustus**.
- **Via Condotti and Via del Corso**: shopping.
- **Quirinale Palace** and the viewpoint over the city.
- **Piazza Barberini** and the **Capuchin Crypt**.
- **Santa Maria della Vittoria**: Bernini&rsquo;s Ecstasy of St Teresa.

## Trastevere and the Janiculum

- **Trastevere**: narrow lanes, trattorie and bars, lively every evening.
- **Santa Maria in Trastevere**: one of the oldest churches in Rome, with golden mosaics.
- **Janiculum Hill (Gianicolo)**: one of the best sunset views of Rome.
- **Villa Farnesina** and **Tiber Island**.
- **Botanical Garden of Rome**.

## Monti, Esquilino and San Lorenzo

- **Monti**: the trendy old neighbourhood with vintage shops and bars.
- **Santa Maria Maggiore**: one of the four great basilicas.
- **San Pietro in Vincoli**: Michelangelo&rsquo;s Moses.
- **San Lorenzo**: the student district, street art and cheap places to eat.

## Aventine, Celio and San Giovanni

- **St John Lateran** and the **Holy Stairs**.
- **Basilica of San Clemente**: three layers of history under one church.
- **Aventine Keyhole**: a keyhole framing St Peter&rsquo;s dome.
- **Orange Garden (Giardino degli Aranci)**: a terrace over Rome.
- **Santa Sabina** and **Rose Garden**.

## Testaccio and Ostiense

- **Testaccio Market** and Roman street food.
- **Pyramid of Cestius** and the **Protestant Cemetery**.
- **Centrale Montemartini**: ancient statues inside a former power plant.
- **Ostiense street art** and the industrial area.
- **Monte Testaccio**: the hill made of ancient pottery. In the evening the area is also where young people go out: see [Rome nightlife]({{ '/rome-nightlife/' | relative_url }}).

## Parks, villas and museums

- **Villa Borghese** and the **Pincio terrace**.
- **Galleria Borghese**: Bernini and Caravaggio (booking required).
- **Villa Doria Pamphili** and **Villa Ada**: the biggest green areas.
- **National Roman Museum**, **Palazzo Barberini**, **Doria Pamphilj Gallery**.
- **MAXXI** and the **Auditorium Parco della Musica**: contemporary Rome.
- **EUR district**: rationalist architecture.
- **Quartiere Coppedè**: a fairy-tale corner of Art Nouveau buildings.
